## Garage Addition Cost: What Wichita Homeowners Are Paying in 2026

Adding a garage to your home is one of the smartest investments you can make — it protects your vehicles, adds usable square footage, and increases resale value. But before you break ground, you need a realistic number to work with.

So what does a garage addition actually cost in Wichita, KS in 2026? Let's break it down honestly.

---

## Average Garage Addition Cost in 2026

Nationally, garage additions run anywhere from **$19,000 to $65,000** depending on size, materials, and whether it's attached or detached. In the Wichita area, you're typically looking at:

- **Single-car attached garage:** $20,000 – $35,000
- **Two-car attached garage:** $32,000 – $55,000
- **Detached single-car garage:** $18,000 – $28,000
- **Detached two-car garage:** $28,000 – $45,000

These ranges reflect real Wichita labor rates, material costs, and permitting — not national averages padded with coastal pricing.

---

## What Drives the Cost of a Garage Addition

### 1. Attached vs. Detached

Attached garages share a wall with your home, which saves on materials but requires more structural coordination — especially if you're tying into an existing foundation or roofline. Detached garages are standalone structures that typically cost less per square foot but require their own utility connections.

### 2. Size and Layout

A standard single-car garage runs about 12×22 ft (264 sq ft). A two-car runs 20×22 ft (440 sq ft) or larger. Every square foot adds cost — but the per-square-foot rate usually drops as you go bigger. If you're already building, going from a one-car to a two-car is often a smarter value move.

### 3. Foundation Type

Most garages in the Wichita area are built on a concrete slab. Slab costs range from **$4 to $8 per square foot** depending on thickness and soil prep. If the site requires grading or drainage work, that adds to the base price.

### 4. Garage Door and Windows

A basic single garage door runs $700–$1,200 installed. A premium insulated door with a smart opener can run $2,500–$4,000+. If you want windows — either in the door or on the sidewalls — budget another $300–$1,200 depending on quantity and style.

### 5. Electrical and Lighting

Most Wichita homeowners want at least basic lighting and outlet circuits in their garage. A standard electrical rough-in and panel connection typically adds **$1,500–$3,500** to the project. If you want EV charging, a 240V outlet adds another $500–$1,200.

### 6. Interior Finish Level

Unfinished drywall and basic paint is the budget route. Insulated walls, epoxy floors, built-in storage, or a finished ceiling pushes costs up — but also pushes up the value and usability of the space significantly.

### 7. Permitting and Inspections

In Sedgwick County and the City of Wichita, garage additions require building permits. Permit fees typically run **$300–$800** for a residential garage. A licensed contractor handles this for you — but it's part of the total cost picture.

---

## Hidden Costs to Watch For

A few line items that often catch homeowners off guard:

- **Tying into existing rooflines** — matching pitch and materials adds labor
- **Utility rerouting** — if gas lines, water lines, or electrical panels are in the way
- **HOA approval** — some Wichita-area neighborhoods require design review before permitting
- **Site grading** — uneven lots or poor drainage can add $1,000–$5,000 before a single board is framed
