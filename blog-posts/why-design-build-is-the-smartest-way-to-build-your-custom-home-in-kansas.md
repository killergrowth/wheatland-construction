When you’re ready to build your dream home, the process should feel exciting—not overwhelming. At Wheatland Construction, we believe the best way to bring your vision to life is through the **Design + Build** method. It’s the approach we’ve used to create stunning custom homes and remodels throughout Butler County and surrounding Kansas communities, and it’s one of the reasons our clients trust us to *build with character*.

## What Is Design + Build?

Unlike the traditional model—where homeowners juggle separate architects, designers, and contractors—the **Design + Build approach combines every step under one roof.** That means from the first sketch to the final blessing of your new space, you’ll work with a single, unified team dedicated to your project.

At Wheatland Construction, that process looks like this:

- **Consultation** – We visit your site, listen to your ideas, and learn your goals.

- **Design** – Our team can create detailed 3D renderings so you can *see* your home before a single wall goes up.

- **Construction** – With skilled craftsmen and clear communication, we bring your plans to life.

- **Handoff** – We celebrate the finished home with a walkthrough and blessing, ensuring everything meets your expectations.

## The Benefits of Design + Build

Choosing a design/build firm like Wheatland Construction offers homeowners several key advantages:

- **One Vision, One Team** – No more miscommunication between designer and builder. Everyone is aligned from day one.

- **Cost Control** – Budgets are managed from the start, reducing costly surprises.

- **Faster Timelines** – With design and construction working hand in hand, projects move smoothly.

- **Higher Quality Results** – Our team is invested in your project from concept to completion.

- **Peace of Mind** – You’ll always know who to call, and you’ll always get an honest answer.

## Why It Matters in Kansas

Building a home in Kansas comes with its own unique considerations—from local zoning requirements to weather-resistant construction. As a family-owned company rooted in Butler County, Wheatland Construction understands these challenges inside and out. Our connections with local suppliers, knowledge of regional codes, and experience with Kansas’ climate help us deliver homes that aren’t just beautiful, but built to last.
