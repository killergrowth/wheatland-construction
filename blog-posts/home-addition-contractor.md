## What Is a Home Addition, Exactly?

A home addition is any project that increases your home's square footage by attaching new construction to the existing structure. That can mean a lot of different things depending on what you need:

- **Room additions** — a bedroom, home office, or playroom added to the main floor
- **Master suite additions** — extra square footage for a larger bedroom, walk-in closet, or ensuite bath
- **In-law suites** — a semi-private living space for a family member
- **Garage conversions or add-ons** — attached garages with finished living space above or adjacent
- **Sunrooms and covered porches** — semi-conditioned spaces that extend your living area seasonally or year-round
- **Second-story additions** — going up instead of out when lot space is limited

Each type has its own structural, permitting, and design challenges. That's why experience matters.

## Common Questions About Home Additions in Wichita

**How long does a home addition take?**
Most additions take 3–6 months from permit approval to completion, depending on size and complexity. Larger or multi-story projects can run longer.

**Do I need a permit for a home addition in Wichita?**
Yes. Any addition that increases your home's footprint requires permits from the City of Wichita. Wheatland handles this process for you.

**Will the addition match my existing home?**
