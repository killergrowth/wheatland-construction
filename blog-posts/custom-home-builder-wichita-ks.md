## Building Something That's Actually Yours

There's a difference between buying a house and building a home. When you buy off a lot or from a production builder, you're picking from someone else's plan — someone else's idea of what a kitchen should look like, how big a master closet needs to be, or whether a mudroom even matters. When you build custom, you start from scratch with your priorities front and center.

Wheatland Construction is a custom home builder serving Wichita, KS and the surrounding area. We work directly with homeowners to plan, design, and construct homes built around real life — not a floor plan catalog.

---

## What "Custom" Actually Means

The word gets thrown around a lot. Here's what it means when you work with Wheatland:

- **Your floor plan.** We're not handing you a binder of pre-drawn options. Your layout is built around how you live — whether that means a home office, a workshop, a mother-in-law suite, or an oversized pantry.
- **Your finishes.** Countertops, cabinetry, flooring, fixtures — you make those calls. We guide you through the options and help you stay on budget without compromising the things that matter most.
- **Your lot, your timeline.** Whether you already own land or need help finding it, we work with your specific site conditions and build around a schedule that makes sense for your situation.

---

## Why Wichita Homeowners Choose Custom

Wichita's housing market has been tight. Inventory in established neighborhoods moves fast, and a lot of what's available in the new construction space is spec-built — meaning someone else decided the layout, the finishes, and the price point. Custom building lets you skip the compromise.

South Wichita, Derby, Andover, Maize, and the surrounding communities all have active custom home builds happening right now. Lot availability varies by area, and site prep costs depend heavily on soil conditions, utilities access, and drainage — all things we assess early so there are no surprises mid-build.

## The Wheatland Process

We keep it straightforward:

### 1. Initial Consultation
We sit down and talk through your vision, your budget, and your timeline. No pressure, no sales pitch — just a real conversation about what you want to build.

### 2. Design & Planning
Working with your architect or ours, we develop a plan that fits your lot and passes permitting. We handle the coordination so you're not bouncing between trades trying to get answers.

### 3. Construction
We manage the full build — foundation through final walkthrough. Framing, mechanical, insulation, drywall, finishes, and landscaping all fall under our oversight. You get consistent communication and a single point of contact throughout.

### 4. Final Walkthrough & Warranty
Before you take keys, we walk the home together. Anything that needs attention gets handled. And after you move in, we stand behind our work.

## What to Budget for a Custom Home in Wichita

Construction costs in Kansas have stabilized after the volatility of the past few years, but material and labor costs still vary depending on your finish level, square footage, and site conditions. A rough starting point for a well-built custom home in the Wichita area currently runs in the range of $150–$250+ per square foot — but that number shifts significantly based on your choices.

The best way to get an accurate number is to talk to us directly. We'll give you a straight answer based on what you actually want to build.
