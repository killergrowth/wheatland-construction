## What Makes a Major Remodel Different

Not all renovation work is created equal. Swapping out light fixtures is one thing. Gutting a kitchen, reconfiguring a floor plan, or converting an unfinished basement into livable space — that's a different level of commitment, planning, and skilled execution.

Major remodels involve:

- **Structural changes** — moving or removing walls, adjusting load-bearing elements
- **Systems coordination** — plumbing, electrical, and HVAC often need to be relocated or updated
- **Permitting and code compliance** — especially important in Sedgwick County and the City of Wichita
- **Extended timelines** — weeks or months, not days, require a contractor you can trust to stay the course

That's why hiring the right home renovation contractor in Wichita, KS matters. Experience with large-scale projects isn't optional — it's the baseline.
