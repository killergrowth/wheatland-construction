## What Is Metal Roofing?

Metal roofing has been protecting commercial buildings for decades, but over the last 10–15 years it's become one of the fastest-growing choices for homeowners across the country—and right here in Wichita, KS. If you're considering a roof replacement or building a new home, metal deserves a serious look.

This guide breaks down everything: the types of metal roofing, the real costs, how long it lasts, and what Kansas weather means for your decision.

---

## Types of Metal Roofing

Not all metal roofs are the same. Here's a quick breakdown of the most common options:

### Standing Seam
The gold standard. Panels run vertically with raised seams that lock together, hiding all fasteners. It's clean, modern, and extremely weather-tight. Most popular for residential installs in Wichita.

### Exposed Fastener Panels (Corrugated / R-Panel)
Lower upfront cost. Fasteners are visible and exposed to the elements, which means they'll need periodic inspection. Common on barns, shops, and budget-conscious installs.

### Metal Shingles & Tiles
Want the look of asphalt or tile without the weight or lifespan limits? Metal shingles mimic traditional profiles while delivering metal-level durability. Great for neighborhoods with HOA restrictions.

### Steel vs. Aluminum vs. Copper
- **Steel** (Galvalume or Galvanized) — most popular, affordable, durable
- **Aluminum** — lightweight, great corrosion resistance, better for coastal or humid climates
- **Copper** — premium, beautiful, lasts 100+ years, significant cost premium

For most Wichita homes, **Galvalume steel** hits the sweet spot of performance and price.

---

## How Long Does a Metal Roof Last?

This is where metal separates itself from the pack.

- **Asphalt shingles:** 15–25 years (often less in Kansas wind and hail)
- **Metal roofing:** 40–70 years, with some premium systems rated for 50+ years with minimal maintenance

In a region like Wichita that sees significant hail, high winds, and temperature swings, that lifespan difference is real money over time.

## Metal Roofing Cost in Wichita, KS

Let's talk numbers. Metal roofing costs more upfront than asphalt—but the math often works out in your favor over time.

**Typical installed cost ranges (Wichita area):**
- Exposed fastener panels: **$6–$9 per sq ft**
- Standing seam: **$10–$16 per sq ft**
- Metal shingles: **$9–$14 per sq ft**

For an average 2,000 sq ft home with a moderately pitched roof, expect a standing seam install to run **$18,000–$30,000** fully installed.

Compare that to asphalt at $8,000–$14,000 — but remember, you'll likely replace asphalt 2–3 times before your metal roof needs attention.

**Other cost factors:**
- Roof pitch and complexity
- Tear-off of existing roof layers
- Decking condition
- Underlayment and trim details

## Pros and Cons of Metal Roofing

### ✅ Pros
- **Longevity** — 40–70+ year lifespan
- **Wind resistance** — rated for 110–160 mph winds (critical in Kansas)
- **Hail resistance** — Class 4 impact rating available (may lower your insurance premium)
- **Energy efficiency** — reflective coatings reduce cooling costs in hot Kansas summers
- **Low maintenance** — no granule loss, no curling, no rotting
- **Eco-friendly** — most metal roofing contains recycled content and is 100% recyclable

### ❌ Cons
- **Higher upfront cost** than asphalt
- **Noise** — rain can be louder without proper insulation (though many homeowners like it)
- **Expansion/contraction** — metal moves with temperature; needs proper fastening system
- **Denting** — large hail can dent softer metals like aluminum

## Metal Roofing and Kansas Weather

Wichita sits in one of the most severe weather zones in the country. That matters when you're picking a roof.

- **Hail:** Kansas averages more hail events than almost any other state. A Class 4 impact-rated metal roof can withstand 2-inch hailstones—and that rating can qualify you for insurance discounts.
- **Wind:** Tornado Alley is real. Standing seam metal roofs with concealed fasteners are among the most wind-resistant residential roofing systems available.
- **Heat:** Wichita summers push 100°F+. Reflective metal coatings can reduce attic temps significantly, cutting your AC load.
- **Ice:** Metal sheds ice and snow faster than asphalt, reducing ice dam risk in winter.

## Is Metal Roofing Right for You?

Metal roofing makes the most sense if:

- You plan to stay in your home 10+ years
- You want to stop replacing your roof every 15–20 years
- You're tired of post-storm repairs
- Energy efficiency matters to you
- You want to potentially lower your homeowner's insurance premium

If you're selling in the next 2–3 years and on a tight budget, asphalt might still be the play. But for most Wichita homeowners thinking long-term, metal is a smart investment.
