## What "Veteran Approved" Actually Means for Home Builders

The term "veteran approved home builder" isn't just a marketing phrase — it has real implications for how your project gets financed and built.

VA construction loans require the builder to be VA-registered and approved. The home must meet VA Minimum Property Requirements (MPRs), and inspections happen at multiple stages of construction. If your builder isn't familiar with these requirements, delays and costly corrections can follow.

Here's what working with a VA-experienced builder in Kansas looks like:

- **Builder registration** — We're registered with the VA so your loan process isn't held up on our end
- **MPR compliance** — Every Wheatland build is designed and constructed to meet VA minimum property standards from the ground up
- **Inspection readiness** — We schedule and prepare for VA inspections at each required milestone so there are no surprises
- **Lender coordination** — We communicate directly with your VA lender to keep draws, timelines, and documentation on track

## Kansas VA Construction Loan — A Quick Overview

If you haven't used a VA construction loan before, here's the short version:

- You must have a **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)** from the VA
- The builder must be **VA-registered**
- The loan typically converts to a permanent VA mortgage at completion
- The home must meet **VA Minimum Property Requirements**

Some lenders offer a single-close construction-to-permanent loan, which simplifies the process significantly. We can connect you with lenders in the Wichita area who specialize in VA construction financing.
