## Home Renovation Mistakes to Avoid: Everything You Need to Know

Starting a major remodel is exciting — until something goes sideways. Whether you're gutting a kitchen, finishing a basement, or adding on square footage, the decisions you make before the first nail goes in can make or break your project. At Wheatland Construction, we've seen Wichita homeowners make the same avoidable mistakes over and over. This guide is here to help you skip the hard lessons.

---

## Mistake #1: Skipping the Planning Phase

One of the most common home renovation mistakes to avoid is diving in without a solid plan. Homeowners get excited, pick out tile, and hire a contractor — all before they've answered the basic questions: What's the scope? What's the timeline? What are the must-haves versus the nice-to-haves?

Before anything else, map out exactly what you want the finished space to look like and what it needs to function as. The more detail you have upfront, the fewer expensive change orders you'll deal with mid-project.

---

## Mistake #2: Underestimating the Budget

This is the one that derails more projects than anything else. Homeowners get a quote, set their budget to that exact number, and leave zero room for the unexpected — and there's always something unexpected.

A good rule of thumb: add a **10–20% contingency buffer** to whatever your contractor quotes you. Hidden water damage, outdated wiring, or supply chain delays can all push costs up fast. In Wichita, material and labor costs have shifted in recent years, so get your quotes current and plan accordingly.

---

## Mistake #3: Hiring Based on Price Alone

We get it — the lowest bid is tempting. But in the construction world, you almost always get what you pay for. A contractor who bids significantly lower than everyone else is usually cutting corners somewhere: cheaper materials, unlicensed subs, skipped permits, or all three.

When vetting contractors for your Wichita remodel, ask for:
- Proof of license and insurance
- Local references you can actually call
- A written contract with scope, timeline, and payment schedule

If they hesitate on any of those, keep looking.

---

## Mistake #4: Ignoring Permits

Pulling permits feels like a hassle. It adds time and a little cost. But skipping them is one of the most expensive home renovation mistakes to avoid in Wichita, KS — full stop.

Unpermitted work can:
- Fail a home inspection when you go to sell
- Void your homeowner's insurance
- Result in fines or forced removal of completed work

Wichita has clear permit requirements for structural changes, electrical, plumbing, and HVAC. A reputable contractor handles this for you. If yours is offering to skip it, that's a red flag.

---

## Mistake #5: Changing the Scope Mid-Project

Scope creep is real, and it's expensive. Once your contractor is mid-demo, it's tempting to say "while we're at it, let's also..." That phrase has added thousands of dollars and weeks of time to countless remodels.

Changes mid-project disrupt scheduling, material orders, and subcontractor timelines. If you want to add something, get it in writing with a formal change order and updated cost before work continues. Don't just hand-shake it.

---

## Mistake #6: Prioritizing Trends Over Timeless

Design trends come and go fast. What's all over Pinterest today can look dated in five years. For a major remodel — especially if you plan to sell eventually — focus on classic, quality finishes that hold their value.

In Wichita's real estate market, buyers respond to clean, neutral, well-built spaces. Bold accent choices are great, but keep the bones of the renovation timeless. Save the trendy stuff for things that are easy and cheap to swap out later.

---

## Mistake #7: Not Vetting Your Contractor's Local Experience

A contractor who's done great work in Kansas City doesn't automatically know Wichita's soil conditions, local code quirks, or which suppliers are reliable here. Local experience matters, especially for larger remodels with structural or foundation components.

Ask directly: *Have you done similar projects in the Wichita area?* A contractor who knows the local landscape will anticipate problems before they become your problems.

---

Major remodels are a significant investment. The best way to protect that investment is to slow down at the start — plan thoroughly, hire carefully, and communicate clearly throughout the project.
