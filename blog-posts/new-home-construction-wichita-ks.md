## New Home Construction in Wichita, KS — What You Should Know Before You Build

If you've been scrolling Zillow and Realtor.com looking at new builds, you already know the problem: most of what's out there is either out of your price range, in a neighborhood that doesn't fit, or cookie-cutter layouts that look like every other house on the block.

New home construction in Wichita, KS doesn't have to work that way.

Building custom means you get to make the calls — layout, finishes, lot location, square footage. You're not buying someone else's vision of what a home should look like. You're building yours.

Here's what you need to know before you get started.

## Why Build Custom Instead of Buying New?

Production builders in the Wichita area build volume. That's their business model. They put up hundreds of homes a year using the same floorplans, the same materials, and the same finishes — just with different addresses.

Custom home construction is the opposite. A local general contractor works with you one project at a time. Your home gets built to your specs, your budget, and your timeline — not pushed through a pipeline designed to maximize someone else's margin.

For buyers in the Wichita market who want something specific — a larger kitchen, a dedicated office, a shop attached to the garage, a walkout basement — custom building is often the only way to get it.

## What the Custom Build Process Actually Looks Like

A lot of people avoid custom home construction because it sounds complicated. It's not — it just has more steps than buying off the shelf. Here's the basic breakdown:

### 1. Land and Lot Selection
You either bring a lot or we help you identify one. Wichita has solid options across the metro — from newer developments in the northwest to established neighborhoods closer to downtown and everything in between.

### 2. Design and Planning
This is where your layout, room count, and features get locked in. We work through plans, finalize the scope, and nail down a realistic budget before a single nail goes in.

### 3. Permitting
Wichita requires building permits for new residential construction. We handle the permitting process with the City of Wichita so you don't have to figure out the paperwork.

### 4. Construction
From foundation to framing to mechanical rough-ins, drywall, and finish work — you'll have a consistent point of contact throughout the build. No getting handed off to someone new every two weeks.

### 5. Final Walkthrough and Delivery
Before we hand over the keys, we do a full walkthrough together to catch anything that needs attention. You move in knowing the work is done right.

## How Long Does It Take to Build a New Home in Wichita?

Timeline depends on size and scope, but most custom single-family homes in the Wichita area run **6 to 12 months** from groundbreak to move-in. Factors that affect the schedule include:

- Square footage and design complexity
- Weather (Kansas winters can slow exterior work)
- Permitting timelines with the City
- Custom material lead times

Starting early and having a clear plan locked in before construction starts is the best way to keep your project on schedule.

---

## What Does New Home Construction Cost in Wichita, KS?

Custom home pricing in Wichita varies significantly based on size, finishes, and site conditions. As a general ballpark:

- **Entry-level custom builds** typically start in the **$150–$200/sq ft** range
- **Mid-range custom homes** land around **$200–$275/sq ft**
- **High-end or complex builds** can run **$300+/sq ft**

These numbers don't include land. The best way to get an accurate number for your specific project is to sit down and talk through scope before you commit to anything.
