## What Does "Custom Home Building" Actually Mean?

There's a big difference between a production builder and a true custom home builder.

**Production builders** offer a catalog. You pick a floor plan, make a few upgrades, and you get a house that looks like the 40 others on your street. Fast, efficient, and fine — if that's what you want.

**Custom home builders** start from your vision. Your lot. Your family's lifestyle. Your must-haves and your non-negotiables. The result is a home designed and built specifically for you — not for a spec sheet.

At Wheatland Construction, we handle custom builds from the ground up. That means:

- Working with your architect or helping you find one
- Navigating lot acquisition and site prep
- Managing every phase of construction — framing, roofing, mechanical, finishes
- Keeping you informed throughout the process (no disappearing acts)

## What to Look for in a Custom Home Builder Near You

Before you sign anything, here's what to evaluate:

### 1. Are They Licensed and Insured?
This is non-negotiable. Ask for proof of general contractor licensing and liability insurance in Kansas.

### 2. Do They Have Local References?
Ask to speak with past clients in the Wichita area. A solid builder will have homeowners willing to talk to you.

### 3. How Do They Handle Communication?
Custom builds take months. You need a builder who communicates clearly, responds promptly, and keeps you updated without you having to chase them down.

### 4. What Does Their Contract Cover?
Get everything in writing — timeline, payment schedule, materials, change order process, and warranty terms.

### 5. Do They Understand Your Budget?
A good builder will help you understand where to invest and where to save — not just tell you what you want to hear.
