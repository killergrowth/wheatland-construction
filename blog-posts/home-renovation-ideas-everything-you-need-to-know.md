## Home Renovation Ideas: Everything You Need to Know

Whether you've been in your Wichita home for two years or twenty, there comes a point when the walls start talking. Maybe the kitchen feels cramped. Maybe the bathrooms are stuck in 1997. Or maybe you're simply ready to invest in the place you call home. Whatever the reason, a major remodel can completely transform how you live — and how much your home is worth.

Here's what you need to know before you pick up a hammer (or hire someone who will).

--

## Start With the Projects That Pay Off

Not all renovations are created equal. Before you dive in, it helps to prioritize projects that deliver real returns — both for daily life and long-term resale value.

### Kitchen Remodels

The kitchen is the heart of almost every Wichita home, and it's consistently one of the highest-ROI renovations you can make. A full kitchen remodel typically includes new cabinetry, countertops, appliances, lighting, and flooring. Even a mid-range kitchen update can recoup 60–80% of its cost at resale — and make every single day better in the meantime.

Key considerations:

- Open layouts continue to dominate — knocking out a non-load-bearing wall can completely change the feel of your main floor

- Quartz and granite countertops remain popular and durable

- Energy-efficient appliances add both function and appeal

### Bathroom Renovations

Outdated bathrooms are one of the top reasons buyers pass on homes. A primary bath remodel — think walk-in shower, double vanity, updated tile — can significantly increase perceived home value and everyday comfort.

Don't overlook secondary bathrooms either. Guest baths and hall bathrooms are often ignored for years, but a relatively modest update can make a big difference.

### Basement Finishing

Here in Wichita, basements are practically a standard feature. Finishing an unfinished basement adds usable square footage — whether that means a home office, a playroom, a home gym, or a guest suite. The cost-per-square-foot is often lower than above-grade additions, making it one of the smartest plays for major remodels.

--

## Whole-Home Renovation: When to Think Bigger

Sometimes room-by-room renovations aren't the right move. If your home has aging systems, a dated floor plan, or structural issues, a comprehensive remodel — or even a full gut renovation — may be more cost-effective in the long run.

Signs you might need a bigger scope:

- HVAC, plumbing, or electrical systems are 20+ years old

- The layout no longer works for your family

- You've already updated cosmetics but the bones still feel wrong

- You're planning to stay in the home for 10+ years

Working with a general contractor who can manage the full scope — permits, subcontractors, timelines, inspections — makes a massive difference on projects like these. In Wichita, navigating city permits and local building codes is part of the job, and having someone experienced in the local market saves headaches.

## Home Renovation Ideas by Room

Need inspiration? Here's a quick hit list:

**Living Room**

- Open up to the kitchen with a wall removal

- Add built-in shelving or entertainment centers

- Install hardwood or luxury vinyl plank flooring

**Primary Bedroom**

- Expand the closet or add a walk-in

- Add tray ceilings or upgraded lighting

- Connect to a remodeled en-suite bathroom

**Exterior / Curb Appeal**

- New siding, windows, or a front porch addition

- Garage door replacement (one of the highest ROI exterior upgrades nationally)

- Landscaping and hardscaping to complement the home

## Budgeting for a Major Remodel in Wichita

Costs vary by scope, materials, and market conditions, but here are rough ranges for Wichita-area projects:

| Project | Estimated Range |
|--|--|
| Kitchen Remodel | $25,000 – $80,000+ |
| Bathroom Remodel | $10,000 – $35,000+ |
| Basement Finish | $20,000 – $50,000 |

Always build in a 10–15% contingency buffer. Surprises happen — especially in older homes.
