Remodeling your home is one of the best ways to add value, improve comfort, and create a space that truly fits your lifestyle. But before you dive into a kitchen upgrade or a whole-home renovation, it’s important to know what to expect. At Wheatland Construction, we’ve guided countless Kansas homeowners through the remodeling process, and we’ve learned what makes a remodel successful—and what can derail one.

Here are the **five most important things to know before remodeling your Kansas home.**

### 1. Set Clear Goals for Your Remodel

Ask yourself: *What do I want to achieve with this remodel?*

- Do you need more space for a growing family?

- Are you updating outdated finishes for resale value?

- Or do you simply want to create your dream kitchen or bathroom?

Having clear goals helps your builder design a plan that matches your vision and your budget.

### 2. Budget Realistically (and Include a Cushion)

Every remodel involves a lot of moving parts—materials, labor, permits, and sometimes hidden surprises once walls are opened. A smart rule of thumb is to budget **10–15% extra** for unexpected costs.

With Wheatland Construction’s **cost-plus contract pricing**, you’ll always see exactly where your money is going—no hidden markups or guesswork.

### 3. Consider Your Home’s Structure and Age

Older homes in Kansas can bring unique challenges, from outdated wiring and plumbing to foundation issues. Before you start picking tile samples, it’s important to have a professional evaluate your home’s structure.

At Wheatland, we perform thorough site visits so you know what’s possible—and what may need to be addressed—before construction begins.

### 4. Plan for Daily Life During Construction

Remodeling can disrupt your normal routine, especially with large projects like kitchen or bathroom renovations. Think about how your family will adjust:

- Will you need a temporary cooking space?

- Should you phase the remodel to limit disruptions?

- Are there certain times of year that work better for your schedule?

Clear communication with your builder makes this process smoother—we keep you updated every step of the way so you can plan accordingly.

### 5. Choose the Right Remodeling Contractor

The most important decision you’ll make is choosing the right partner. Look for:

- **Local experience** in Butler County and surrounding Kansas communities

- **Transparency** in pricing and communication

- **Credentials** (like being Licensed & Insured, HomeAdvisor Certified, and a Veterans Approved Builder)

- **Values you can trust**—at Wheatland, we build with character, guided by faith and integrity.
