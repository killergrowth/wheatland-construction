## Custom Home Building Checklist: Everything You Need to Know

Building a custom home in Wichita, KS is one of the biggest investments you'll ever make — and one of the most rewarding. But without a clear plan, the process can feel overwhelming fast. This custom home building checklist breaks down every major phase so you know exactly what to expect and what to prepare for, from your first conversation with a builder to the day you get your keys.

---

## Phase 1: Pre-Construction Planning

Before a single nail gets driven, there's a lot of groundwork to lay.

### Define Your Budget
Start with a realistic number. Factor in land costs, construction, permits, design fees, and a contingency buffer (typically 10–15% of your total build budget). Wichita land prices vary significantly by area — northwest and east side lots will run different numbers, so research early.

### Choose Your Lot
Not all land is build-ready. Before purchasing, confirm:
- Zoning compatibility for your intended home type
- Availability of utilities (water, sewer, electric, gas)
- Soil conditions and drainage
- HOA restrictions, if applicable

### Select Your Builder
In Wichita, you'll find everything from large volume builders to custom general contractors. If you want full design control, work with a custom builder who can bring your vision to life from the ground up rather than choosing from a preset floor plan catalog.

### Nail Down Your Design
Work with an architect or your builder's design team to finalize:
- Floor plan and square footage
- Exterior style and materials
- Roof type
- Garage configuration
- Special features (bonus rooms, covered patios, basements, etc.)

---

## Phase 2: Permits and Approvals

Once your plans are finalized, your builder will pull the required permits from the City of Wichita. This typically includes a building permit, electrical permit, plumbing permit, and mechanical permit. Permit timelines in Sedgwick County can range from a few days to a few weeks depending on project complexity, so build this into your schedule.

---

## Phase 3: Site Prep and Foundation

This is where the physical work begins.

**Site prep checklist:**
- Land clearing and grading
- Utility connections roughed in
- Surveying and staking
- Erosion control measures

**Foundation checklist:**
- Foundation type confirmed (slab, crawl space, or full basement — basements are common in Wichita due to tornado safety)
- Footings poured and inspected
- Foundation walls formed and cured
- Waterproofing applied where needed

---

## Phase 4: Framing, Rough-Ins, and Inspections

Once the foundation passes inspection, framing begins. This is when your home starts to look like a home.

**Framing:**
- Exterior walls, interior walls, and roof structure

**Rough-ins (completed before drywall):**
- Electrical wiring
- Plumbing supply and drain lines
- HVAC ductwork
- Insulation

Each trade rough-in typically requires a city inspection before walls are closed up. Your builder should be coordinating all of this — but it's worth confirming inspection sign-offs at each stage.

---

## Phase 5: Finishes and Interior Work

This phase is where your choices really show up.

**Exterior finishes:**
- Roofing, siding, windows, and doors installed
- Exterior paint or stain applied
- Driveway and walkways poured

**Interior finishes:**
- Drywall hung, taped, and painted
- Flooring installed (hardwood, tile, LVP — your call)
- Cabinets and countertops set
- Plumbing fixtures, light fixtures, and hardware installed
- Trim, doors, and millwork completed

---

## Phase 6: Final Inspections and Punch List

Before move-in, your home goes through a final city inspection. Once that passes, you and your builder will do a walkthrough to create a **punch list** — a written list of any items that need touch-up or correction. Common punch list items include:

- Paint touch-ups
- Cabinet adjustments
- Grout or caulk gaps
- Fixture alignment

Get everything in writing and confirm completion before you close.

---

## Phase 7: Move-In Prep

**Before you move in:**
- Confirm all utilities are active and in your name
- Change locks and program garage openers
- Review your builder's warranty coverage
- Document the home with photos for insurance purposes
- Schedule final landscaping if not included in the build
