## Bathroom Remodel Cost: What to Expect in 2026

If you've been putting off a bathroom remodel because you're not sure what it's going to cost, you're not alone. It's one of the most common questions we get from Wichita homeowners — and honestly, one of the hardest to answer with a single number. The truth is, bathroom remodel costs in 2026 vary widely depending on the size of the space, the materials you choose, and how much of the layout you're changing.

This guide breaks it all down so you can walk into the planning process with realistic expectations and no surprises.

## The National Average — and What It Means for Wichita

Nationally, bathroom remodels run anywhere from **$6,000 to $35,000+**, with the average full remodel landing around **$10,000–$15,000**. High-end master bath renovations can push $50,000 or more.

Here in Wichita, KS, you'll generally land on the lower end of national averages — labor costs are more reasonable here than in coastal markets, and many local suppliers offer competitive pricing on materials. That said, 2025 and 2026 have brought continued pressure from material costs, so budgets that worked two or three years ago may need to be revised upward.

## Cost Breakdown by Scope

### Basic Refresh ($3,000–$7,000)

This covers cosmetic upgrades with no structural changes — new fixtures, updated lighting, fresh paint, a new vanity, and possibly new flooring. If your layout works and your plumbing is solid, this is the fastest path to a dramatically improved bathroom.

### Mid-Range Remodel ($8,000–$20,000)

This is the most common range for Wichita homeowners doing a real overhaul. Expect new tile throughout, a new tub or shower enclosure, updated plumbing fixtures, vanity replacement, and potentially reconfiguring the layout slightly. This is also where you'll add features like a tile shower with a bench, niche shelving, or upgraded lighting packages.

### Major / High-End Remodel ($20,000–$50,000+)

Full gut-to-studs renovations, master bath expansions, custom tile work, freestanding tubs, radiant floor heating, steam showers, and premium fixture brands all push costs into this tier. If you're converting a standard bath into a spa-level master suite, plan accordingly.

--

## What Drives the Price Up

Not all bathrooms are created equal. Here are the biggest cost drivers:

- **Tile selection and square footage** — Custom or large-format tile takes more time and skill to install. Intricate patterns cost more in labor.

- **Plumbing moves** — Moving a toilet, tub, or shower drain is expensive. Keeping fixtures in their current footprint saves money.

- **Fixture and material grade** — Big box vs. specialty brands can be a $5,000 swing on fixtures alone.

- **Shower type** — A prefab shower insert is cheaper than a custom tile shower with a frameless glass enclosure.

- **Structural issues** — Older Wichita homes sometimes reveal water damage, subfloor rot, or outdated plumbing once demo begins. Always build a contingency buffer (10–15%) into your budget.

## How to Budget Smart

- **Start with your must-haves.** Know what you can't live without before you start pricing out nice-to-haves.

- **Get at least two or three quotes.** Wichita has solid contractors — compare apples to apples and ask what's included.

- **Don't skip the contingency.** Budget 10–15% above your quote for surprises. It's not pessimism; it's experience.

- **Think long-term.** A well-done bathroom remodel adds real resale value in the Wichita market. Cheap shortcuts show up on inspection reports.
