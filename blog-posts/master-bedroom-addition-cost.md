## Master Bedroom Addition Cost: What to Expect in 2026

Adding a master bedroom to your home is one of the most impactful investments you can make — both for your comfort and your property value. But before you break ground, you need a realistic number in your head. Whether you're a growing family, planning for aging parents, or just done sharing space, understanding master bedroom addition costs in Wichita, KS will help you plan smart and avoid surprises.

Here's a straight-forward breakdown of what you're actually looking at in 2026.

---

## Average Cost of a Master Bedroom Addition

Nationally, a master bedroom addition runs anywhere from **$80 to $200+ per square foot**, depending on complexity, finishes, and local labor rates. In Wichita, you're typically looking at:

- **Basic addition (200–300 sq ft):** $25,000–$55,000
- **Mid-range with ensuite bath:** $60,000–$100,000
- **High-end with walk-in closet, custom finishes, full bath:** $100,000–$160,000+

These are real-world ranges based on current material and labor costs. Don't let national "average" figures mislead you — Wichita pricing is generally more favorable than coastal metros, but material costs have climbed industry-wide since 2022 and are holding firm in 2026.

---

## What Drives the Cost Up (or Down)

### Size of the Addition
Bigger square footage means more materials and more labor hours. A 200 sq ft addition and a 400 sq ft addition aren't just twice the cost — the structural complexity often scales faster than the size.

### Foundation Type
Slab-on-grade is typically the most cost-efficient. If your addition requires a crawl space or full basement tie-in, expect that to add $10,000–$25,000 or more depending on soil conditions and grade.

### Roof Tie-In
How your new addition connects to your existing roofline is a major cost driver. A simple shed roof addition is less expensive than matching a complex hip or gable roof on an established home.

### Ensuite Bathroom
Most master bedroom additions include a private bath — and that's where costs jump. A standard ensuite adds $15,000–$35,000 to your project. Custom tile, dual vanities, a soaking tub? Plan accordingly.

### Walk-In Closet
Often bundled with the master suite, a walk-in closet adds square footage and sometimes custom built-ins. Budget an additional $3,000–$10,000 depending on what's inside it.

### Permit and Engineering
In Wichita, additions require permits through the City of Wichita's Development Services. Structural engineering, plan review, and inspections typically add $2,000–$5,000. A good contractor handles this for you — if they're not pulling permits, walk away.

## What's Included in a Full Master Bedroom Addition Build

When you hire a full-service general contractor for your addition, here's what the scope typically covers:

- Site prep and foundation
- Framing (walls, floor, roof)
- Exterior siding matched to existing home
- Insulation and drywall
- Electrical (outlets, lighting, ceiling fan)
- HVAC extension or new zone
- Windows and exterior door (if applicable)
- Flooring
- Interior trim and paint
- Bathroom rough-in and finish (if included)

Every one of those line items is a cost variable. Material selections matter — luxury vinyl plank vs. hardwood, builder-grade fixtures vs. custom, big-box windows vs. commercial-grade.

## Does a Master Bedroom Addition Add Value?

Yes — but the ROI depends on execution. A well-built master suite addition in Wichita typically recoups 50–70% of its cost at resale. More importantly, it adds livable square footage to your home's appraised value, which matters for refinancing and long-term equity.

The key word is *well-built*. A poorly executed addition — mismatched exterior, inadequate HVAC, or code violations — can hurt value instead of help it.
