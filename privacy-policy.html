Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 1, 2026

Wheatland Construction (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you visit our website at wheatlandconstruction.com.

Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Contact Information: Name, phone number, email address, and project details you submit through our contact or project request forms.
  • Usage Data: Pages visited, time spent on pages, browser type, and referring URLs, collected automatically through Google Analytics.
  • Location Data: General geographic location based on IP address.

How We Use Your Information

  • To respond to your inquiries and project requests
  • To communicate about our services
  • To improve our website and user experience
  • To analyze site traffic via Google Analytics

Google Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors use our site. Google Analytics collects data such as pages visited, time on site, and general location. This data is aggregated and anonymous. You can opt out of Google Analytics by installing the Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on.

Cookies

Our website may use cookies to enhance your browsing experience. You can instruct your browser to refuse cookies or alert you when cookies are being sent. Some features of the site may not function properly without cookies.

Third-Party Services

We use Cloudflare for website security and performance. Cloudflare may collect certain data as part of its service. Please review Cloudflare’s Privacy Policy for details.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. We may share data with trusted service providers (Google, Cloudflare) solely to operate our website and services.

Data Retention

We retain contact form submissions for as long as necessary to respond to your inquiry or as required by law. Google Analytics data is retained per Google’s standard retention policies.

Your Rights

You have the right to request access to, correction of, or deletion of any personal information we hold about you. To make a request, contact us at contact@wheatlandconstruction.com.

Children’s Privacy

Our website is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy
