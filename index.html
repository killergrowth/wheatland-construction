Our Process

1

Consultation

We begin by reviewing your ideas, existing plans, or needed drawings and discuss your goals and budget. Let’s outline the next steps to bring your project to life!

2

Contracting

Once the scope is clear, we present a detailed proposal and offer flexible pricing options — whether you prefer a cost-plus or hard bid arrangement — to fit your needs and budget.

3

Pre-Construction

Let’s finalize the details — selecting materials, refining the schedule, etc. This step ensures clarity and confidence before we break ground. Measure twice, cut once!

4

Construction

Our dedicated staff are involved throughout the construction process. We communicate well with you throughout, ensuring your vision is executed to your expectations.

5

Completion

Our job isn’t done until everything meets our high standards — and yours. When the project is complete, we hope we’ve turned you into a true fan. We finish by praying for peace and joy in your new or updated home for years to come!
